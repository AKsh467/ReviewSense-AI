Project: ReviewSense AI
Experiment: E3 – Refined Prompt
Prompt Version: V3
Platform: ChatGPT
Model: Not verified from interface
Dataset: 4 headphone reviews
Settings: Default chat settings

1. OBJECTIVE

Evaluate whether V3 improves analysis quality by reducing redundancy, enforcing consistent priority rules, separating positive and negative review counts, and producing concise evidence-based product recommendations.

2. OBSERVED STRENGTHS

- Correctly classified overall sentiment as Mixed.
- Identified relevant product aspects and their sentiments.
- Reported total, positive, and negative review counts for each aspect.
- Correctly identified Battery Life as the recurring negative concern.
- Distinguished multiple-review concerns from single-review concerns.
- Applied priority rules consistently: Battery Life as High priority and Comfort and Price as Medium priority.
- Provided actionable recommendations directly linked to negative customer feedback.
- Produced valid structured JSON output.

3. OBSERVED WEAKNESSES

- Some information still overlaps between aspect analysis, recurring concerns, and product strengths.
- Supporting quotations were not exhaustive for all aspects, although the prompt required only one or two quotes.
- The JSON output requires validation before integration into the working application.
- Prioritization is based on review frequency alone and does not consider business impact or issue severity.

5. CONCLUSION

V3 provides a more decision-oriented and consistent analysis compared with earlier prompt versions. It improves the separation of positive and negative feedback, applies predefined priority rules consistently, and connects customer complaints directly to actionable recommendations.

Some redundancy remains, and frequency-based prioritization does not account for business impact or severity. Nevertheless, V3 demonstrates improvement in consistency and practical usefulness for product review analysis.

V3 will be considered a strong candidate for the final prompt. Its selection will be confirmed after comparative evaluation of V1, V2, and V3.
