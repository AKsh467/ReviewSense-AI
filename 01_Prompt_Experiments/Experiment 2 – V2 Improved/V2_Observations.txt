Prompt Version: V2

Settings: Default chat settings

1. OBJECTIVE

Evaluate whether V2 improves the baseline analysis by adding review frequency, recurring theme identification, evidence-based analysis, prioritization, and structured output.

2. OBSERVED STRENGTHS

- Correctly identified overall sentiment as Mixed.
- Identified five product aspects and classified their sentiments.
- Counted distinct reviews mentioning each aspect.
- Separated recurring themes from single-review themes.
- Provided exact supporting quotations.
- Separated product strengths and customer pain points.
- Generated prioritized improvement suggestions.
- Returned structured JSON output.

3. OBSERVED WEAKNESSES

- Findings were repeated across multiple sections, including aspect analysis, recurring themes, strengths, and pain points.
- Comfort received High priority despite negative feedback appearing in only one review.
- Priority levels were provided without clearly defined prioritization criteria.
- The output could be more concise by reducing redundant information across sections.

5. CONCLUSION

V2 improved upon V1 by introducing quantitative theme frequency, distinguishing recurring themes from single-review observations, and providing structured JSON output with prioritized improvement suggestions.

However, some findings were repeated across sections and improvement priorities were not consistently supported by clearly defined criteria.

V3 should focus on reducing redundant output, improving prioritization consistency, and making the analysis more concise while retaining the strengths introduced in V2.
